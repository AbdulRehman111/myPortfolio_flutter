Case study

smartFUJAIRAH

A comprehensive digital government mobile platform for Fujairah Municipality, unifying services, payments, land, documents and notifications.

Stack: Flutter · Dart · Firebase · REST · JWT · Riverpod · GoRouter · Google Maps · Apple Pay · Google Pay · N-Genius

Problem

Citizens needed a single, bilingual mobile channel for municipal requests, tracking, land services and payments instead of fragmented portals.

Approach

  • Flutter client with RTL/LTR Arabic and English layout.
  • JWT authentication and REST integration with municipal APIs.
  • Wallet and payment gateways including Apple Pay, Google Pay and N-Genius.
  • Maps, document upload/download, and Firebase messaging.

Outcome

Published on App Store and Google Play as the official smartFUJAIRAH application, supporting continuous feature delivery and production support.
